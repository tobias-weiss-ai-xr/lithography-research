# Lithography Research — Detailed Facts

## Article Summary (heise.de, 2026-07-28)

**"Chip-Lithografie: China nähert sich angeblich der Serienreife"**
Author: Mark Mantel

### What Happened

- The Information reported China is mass-producing homegrown DUV chipmaking tools
- Reuters followed, naming **Shanghai Aishengna Electronic Technology Group** as the manufacturer
- ASML's stock dropped **12%** on the news

### About Aishengna

- State-owned enterprise
- Founded: August 2023
- Linked to Yuliangsheng (same company address)
- Yuliangsheng was the subject of similar rumors in **2025** (prototype DUV immersion)
- 2026 plan: **5 DUV immersion systems**
- 2027 plan: **20 DUV immersion systems**

### DUV Immersion Technology

- **193nm** argon fluoride (ArF) excimer laser
- Immersion = ultrapure water between final lens and wafer
- Water refracts light → improves resolution
- Used for nodes from 16nm down to 7nm (with multi-patterning)
- Not sufficient for 5nm and below (needs EUV at 13.5nm)

### Customers

- **SMIC** (Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corp) — China's largest foundry
- **Huawei** — in-house chip design
- **Hua Hong** — foundry
- **CXMT** (ChangXin Memory Technologies) — DRAM manufacturer

### Critical Open Questions

1. **Resolution** — what min feature size can it actually achieve?
2. **Throughput** — wafers per hour?
3. **Overlay accuracy** — multi-patterning requires atomic-scale alignment
4. **Yield** — percentage of functional dies per wafer?
5. **Production volume** — 5 systems vs. ASML's hundreds per year

### ASML's Advantage

- Decades of precision engineering experience
- Current systems align wafers with **atomic-scale precision**
- Overlay accuracy measured in **sub-nanometer** range
- Only supplier of EUV (13.5nm) and High-NA EUV systems

### Chinese Path to Advanced Nodes (Current)

1. Bought ASML DUV systems before export restrictions tightened
2. Use **multi-patterning** to achieve 7nm-level features with DUV
3. Multi-patterning = expose same layer 3-4x → reduces throughput and yield
4. Only viable due to state subsidies
5. TSMC abandoned similar approach for 7nm due to economic constraints

### Chinese EUV Aspirations

- Chinese physicists published papers calling for domestic EUV development
- Unconfirmed reports of Chinese EUV prototype systems
- ASML's EUV monopoly remains unchallenged for foreseeable future
- EUV requires:
  - Ultra-high-power LPP (laser-produced plasma) tin droplet source (250W+)
  - Complex multi-layer reflective optics (Mo/Si mirrors)
  - Vacuum environment
  - Extremely precise reticle and wafer stages
