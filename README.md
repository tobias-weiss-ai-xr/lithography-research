## Global Lithography Landscape

### Key Players

| Company | HQ | DUV | EUV | High-NA EUV |
|---------|----|-----|-----|-------------|
| **ASML** | Netherlands | Dominant (1980i, 2000i, 2050i, 2100i) | Only supplier (NXE:3800E) | Only supplier (EXE:5200) |
| **Canon** | Japan | NIL, DUV (FPA-6300ES) | No | No |
| **Nikon** | Japan | DUV (NSR-S636E) | No | No |
| **Aishengna** | China | DUV Immersion (prototype/early production) | No | No |

### Technology Nodes vs. Lithography

| Node | Lithography Required |
|------|---------------------|
| ≥ 28nm | DUV dry (193nm) |
| 16nm/14nm | DUV immersion (193nm + water) |
| 10nm | DUV immersion + multi-patterning |
| 7nm | DUV immersion + multi-patterning, or EUV |
| 5nm | EUV (13.5nm) |
| 3nm | EUV + multi-patterning |
| 2nm | High-NA EUV |

## Chinese Domestic Lithography Progress

### Key Players

1. **Shanghai Aishengna Electronic Technology Group (Aishengna)**
   - State-owned, founded August 2023
   - Linked to Yuliangsheng (same address)
   - **DUV Immersion systems** reported in prototype since 2025
   - Plan: 5 systems in 2026, 20 in 2027
   - Potential customers: SMIC, Huawei, Hua Hong, CXMT

2. **Yuliangsheng**
   - Rumored DUV immersion prototype since 2025
   - Target: 28nm chips, finer with multi-patterning
   - Same address as Aishengna

### Current Chinese Capabilities

**Working:**
- DUV immersion technology in late prototype / early production
- Multi-patterning on ASML-sourced DUV systems (7nm at SMIC)
- Growing ecosystem of local suppliers

**Key unknowns:**
- Actual resolution achieved
- Wafer throughput (wafers/hour)
- Overlay accuracy (alignment precision)
- Yield rates
- Reliability

**Chinese physicists have acknowledged** the need for domestic EUV development, publishing papers on EUV source technology.

### Limitations

- Even 20 systems/year would not replace ASML installed base in China
- Only DUV immersion, no EUV capability
- Immature precision alignment vs. ASML's atomic-scale alignment
- Export controls on ASML systems continue to tighten
- Multi-patterning with DUV for 7nm limits yield and economics

## Export Controls

- ASML has **never** been allowed to sell EUV systems to China
- Since 2023: ASML also restricted on advanced DUV immersion systems
- Since 2024: Even older DUV systems require export licenses
- US/Netherlands/Japan (Chip 4) coordinate restrictions

## Other Developments

- Reports (unconfirmed) of Chinese EUV research prototypes
- ASML increasing production capacity: aiming for significantly more systems
- Canon pursuing Nanoimprint Lithography (NIL) as alternative
- Chinese physicists publishing on EUV source technology
